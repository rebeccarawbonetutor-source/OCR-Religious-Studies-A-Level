Religion and Ethics

Natural Law →

Aquinas: four tiers of law, primary and secondary precepts, real and apparent goods, double effect. Challenges from proportionalism.

Situation Ethics →

Fletcher: agape as the only norm, the six propositions and four working principles. Challenges from Weber and Vardy.

Kantian Ethics →

The categorical and hypothetical imperatives, duty, the three postulates, and universalisability. Challenges from Bentley and Gardiner.

Utilitarianism →

Bentham's hedonistic calculus, Mill's rule utilitarianism and higher pleasures, Singer's preference and act utilitarianism.

Euthanasia →

Sanctity of life, quality of life, personhood; voluntary, non-voluntary and involuntary euthanasia; applied to the four normative theories.

Business Ethics →

Corporate social responsibility, whistle-blowing, good and bad practice; applied to the four normative theories and to human nature theories.

Sexual Ethics →

Attitudes to pre- and extra-marital sex and homosexuality; religious and secular views; applied to the four normative theories.

Meta-Ethics →

Cognitive and non-cognitive theories; naturalism and emotivism (Ayer, Stevenson), prescriptivism (Hare); Hume's is-ought gap.

Conscience →

Aquinas: ratio, synderesis and conscientia; Freud: the super-ego and guilt. Secular vs religious understandings compared.

Free Will and Determinism →

Hard and soft determinism, libertarianism; theological determinism and predestination; scientific and psychological determinism.
