Developments in Christian Thought

Augustine's Teaching on Human Nature →

Genesis 3 and the Fall, friendship before and lust after; Original Sin, its effects on the will and societies; only God's grace can overcome sin.

Death and the Afterlife →

Judgement, heaven and hell, purgatory; election — limited, unlimited and universalist; the parable of the sheep and the goats.

Knowledge of God's Existence →

Natural knowledge through reason and empiricism (Calvin, Romans 1); revealed knowledge through grace and faith (Barth); their relative sufficiency.

The Person of Jesus Christ →

Jesus as Son of God, teacher of wisdom, liberator; his authority, resurrection and divinity — the views of Bultmann, Hick and John's Gospel.

Christian Moral Principles →

Dominant secular vs Christian values; the Bible as a moral guide; agape, reason and conscience — whether Christian ethics are distinctive.

Christian Moral Action →

Bonhoeffer: obedience and civil disobedience, suffering, and the cost of discipleship; whether his theology has relevance today.

Gender and Theology →

Gender, God and feminist theology; Ruether and Daly on patriarchy, whether Christianity can be changed or should be abandoned.

The Challenge of Secularism →

Secularisation and its causes; God as illusion (Freud, Dawkins); Christianity and public life; responses from believers.

Liberation Theology and Marx →

Marx's influence, sin as structural injustice, praxis and orthopraxy; Romero and the option for the poor; criticisms from the Magisterium.
