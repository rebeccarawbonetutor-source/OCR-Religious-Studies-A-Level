Philosophy of Religion

Paper 1 • H573/01 — Specification Topics

Ancient Philosophical Influences →

Plato: reality, the Forms, the analogy of the cave. Aristotle: the four causes, Prime Mover. Their contrasting reliance on reason and the senses.

Soul, Mind and Body →

Plato and Aristotle on the soul; Descartes' substance dualism; materialist responses — Dawkins, Ryle, identity theory and functionalism.

Arguments Based on Observation →

The teleological argument (Aquinas' Fifth Way, Paley) and the cosmological argument (Aquinas' first three ways); Hume and the challenge of evolution.

Arguments Based on Reason →

The ontological argument: Anselm, Descartes and Malcolm; challenges from Gaunilo and Kant on existence as a predicate.

Religious Experience →

The nature and influence of religious experience: mystical and conversion experiences; Otto, James and Teresa; the three interpretations; the argument from experience.

The Problem of Evil →

Logical and evidential problems; the free will defence and Augustine's theodicy; Hick's vale of soul-making; challenges from Mackie and Plantinga.

The Nature or Attributes of God →

God as omnipotent, omniscient, omnibenevolent and eternal; Boethius and Anselm on God and time; challenges from Swinburne, Kenny and process thought.

Religious Language →

Via negativa and analogical language (Pseudo-Dionysius, Aquinas, Ramsey); symbol and myth (Tillich); verification and falsification; language games.
